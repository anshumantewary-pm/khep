The solution on one page

Indian trucking is mostly small owners with a few trucks each, connected to shippers through layers of commission agents. Money moves late, prices are opaque, return trips run empty and drivers carry the cost of long waits. Khep fixes the money first, because guaranteed payment is what pulls small owners off the broker network. Everything else builds on the trip data that payment flow creates.

  • Escrow-backed loads

    Shipper funds the freight before posting. 90% goes to the owner by UPI at loading, the rest on e-POD. Deductions need photo proof within 24 hours.

  • Fair lane rates

    A price band per lane and truck type from completed trips, shown to both sides. Ends the phone-around for quotes.

  • Return-load matching

    Back-haul offered for the truck's arrival time and city while the forward trip is still running.

  • Paperwork on the trip

    E-way bill from the invoice, digital LR/bilty, e-POD with consignee OTP, vehicle and licence checks, expiry alerts.

  • Driver-first app

    Hindi and regional languages, voice, one big button per step, auto-billed detention, rest stops with safe parking.

  • Credit from trip history

    Fuel and toll credit lines and invoice discounting, underwritten on the owner's Khep payment record.

Rollout

  1. One corridor. Punjab and Delhi NCR to Bhiwandi/Mumbai. 50 mid-size shippers, 500 trucks, full-truckload only. Prove escrow and the 90% advance.
  2. Return loads and credit. Add back-haul matching once both ends of the corridor have shippers. Launch fuel credit using six months of payment history.
  3. Five corridors. Add the busiest lanes out of Gujarat, Chennai and Kolkata. Add Tamil, Telugu, Bengali and Marathi to the driver app.

What has to be true, and how to test it

  • Shippers will pre-fund. Most pay on 30–90 day credit today. Test: offer a lower rate (the owner's saved commission passed back) in exchange for escrow, and count how many of 50 shippers accept.
  • Owners leave their agents. Agents also give credit and fill gaps. Test: track repeat bookings per owner after the first paid trip.
  • Drivers will use it. Test the five-step trip screen with 20 drivers at a transport nagar, by voice and by tap, before writing more features.
  • The rate band is trusted. It needs enough trips per lane. Seed it from shipper historical data until volume builds.

This plan was drafted from general knowledge of the Indian trucking market, not from the five research threads, which were not available in this session. Figures such as agent commission, payment terms and empty-running share are commonly cited industry estimates and should be checked against that research.
