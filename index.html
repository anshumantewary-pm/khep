    Questions

    Asked at every dhaba

    Is Khep live yet?

    Khep is in early access. The app works today, but loads, trucks and prices in it are samples while we bring on our first partners on the Punjab and Delhi NCR to Mumbai route.

    Is it free?

    Downloading and signing up is free. When payments go live, truck owners will pay a flat 2% per trip, instead of the 5–10% that agents usually take.

    How do drivers get paid?

    The company pays into escrow before the trip. 90% goes to the owner at loading, and the rest when the receiver gives the delivery code. Deductions need photos within 24 hours. Read the full guide.

    Why does Android warn me when I install it?

    Google Play Protect warns about any app that isn't from the Play Store. Tap More details, then Install anyway. The warning goes away once Khep is on the Play Store.

    Does Khep track my location?

    Only if you allow it, and only to sort loads, trucks and transporters by distance. In this version your location stays on your phone.

    Is there an iPhone app?

    Open this website in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Khep then works like an app. An App Store version comes later.

    Which languages does it support?

    Khep uses Hindi alongside English in key places today. Full Hindi, Punjabi, Marathi, Gujarati, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali and Kannada are on the way.
